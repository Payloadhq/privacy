# Privacy Policy — AI Search Readiness Audit (Chrome Extension)
Product: AI Search Readiness Audit, v1.0.0 — Manifest V3 browser extension by Payload.
Effective date: October 2026.

## Short version

AI Search Readiness Audit collects, stores, and transmits nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser.

## What the extension does with your data

When you run an audit, the extension fetches the raw HTML of the page you are viewing and that site's robots.txt, then analyzes them inside your browser to produce the audit report. The analyzed content never leaves your device.

Specifically:

- **No personal data collected.** The extension does not collect names, email addresses, browsing history, or any other personal information.
- **No data transmitted.** The extension sends nothing to any server — not ours, not anyone else's. Its only network requests are (1) loading its own bundled files, (2) fetching the raw HTML of the page you chose to audit, and (3) fetching that site's robots.txt.
- **No analytics or tracking.** There are no analytics SDKs, no telemetry, no cookies, no fingerprinting.
- **Local storage only.** The current audit report is kept temporarily in the browser's session storage so the report tab can display it. It is cleared when the session ends. Nothing is written to a remote server.

## Permissions used and why

- **Host access (all sites):** needed to fetch the raw HTML and robots.txt of whichever page you choose to audit.
- **Storage:** needed to hold the current audit report temporarily in session storage.

## Third parties

None. No data is shared with any third party, because no data is collected.
